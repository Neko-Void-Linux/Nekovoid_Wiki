## Why GPL-3.0?

We chose the GNU General Public License v3.0 because we believe in:

1. **Copyleft (Share Alike):** It guarantees that any modifications or derivatives of our operating system must also be released under the same open-source terms, protecting the community's freedom.
2. **User Rights:** It explicitly protects users against Tivoization (hardware restrictions preventing modified software from running) and discriminatory patent grants.
3. **Collaboration:** As a Linux distribution built upon many other GPL-licensed tools, adopting the GPLv3 ensures maximum compatibility and encourages a strong, collaborative ecosystem where improvements are shared back to the source.
