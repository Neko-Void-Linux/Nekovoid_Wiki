## What is Neko Void?

Neko Void is more than just a distribution; it is a **community-driven open source solution** designed to bring a reliable, lightweight, and pre-configured Linux experience to your computer.

### Built by the Community, for the Community

Neko Void bridges the gap between the power of Void Linux and the need for an accessible, ready-to-use desktop environment. Our mission is to foster a collaborative environment where developers and users work together to maintain compatibility and performance on Linux.

* **Powered by Collaboration:** Neko Void leverages the incredible work of the open-source community, ensuring that your system remains updated and stable.
* **Open & Transparent:** Developed under open licenses, our codebase is fully transparent. We encourage users to contribute, whether through code, translations, or bug reporting.
* **Standardized Distribution:** By utilizing native repositories, we ensure that the community’s efforts result in a secure, isolated, and consistent experience.

> **Join the Project:** Neko Void is a living ecosystem. Every improvement you see is the result of community feedback and the shared goal of making Linux accessible to everyone.
